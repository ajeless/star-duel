#STAR-DUEL

## DESCRIPTION
A 2 player, top down, ship-to-ship, space combat simulator. Turn based combat, on a hexagonal grid/background.

## UI/UX
- 2 players share the same keyboard, alternating turns
- HUD display player 1 left side screen, player 2 right side screen
  * Torpedoes left
  * Shield health
  * Hull health



## Ships Weapons and Systems
- Photon Torpedoes - integer quantity and damage
- Hull Integrity - integer
- Shields - integer
- Engines - x hex movement path plotted by player



## Actions Allowed Per Turn
Limit 2 per player per turn
- Raise/Lower shields
- fire torpedos
- Move

You have to lower shields to fire fire torpedoes
